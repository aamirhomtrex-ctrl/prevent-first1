See what your team needs, without seeing anyone's health data

Prevent First for Business shows group-level needs, such as how many drivers are due a blood pressure check, and drafts the programme. No individual is ever shown. Small groups are hidden.

Use your own company's numbers

One row per team: group name, number of people, then how many in that team fit each need. Use totals only, never names. Copy them from a spreadsheet or a health-camp report and paste below. Commas, semicolons or tabs all work.

Invite your team, one message for everyone

You never receive anyone's health status, and you cannot send it for them. Instead, send everyone the same invitation. Each person opens the link, answers a few questions on their own phone and sees their own private plan. They can then choose to add one anonymous line to their team's totals.

Load team responses

Paste the "published to the web" CSV link of the Google Sheet that collects the team codes. The page adds them up by team. Percentages are of the people who responded, and small teams stay hidden.

What the employer sees, and what they never see

Employer sees
  • Counts for a group, such as “14 of 22 drivers are due a blood pressure check”.
  • Suggested programmes, drafted for a person at the company to approve.
  • Nothing for groups under the minimum size.
Employer never sees
  • Names, ages, test results or answers of any person.
  • Any count smaller than the minimum, shown as “<5”.
  • Which individual is in a group of one.

Each employee gets their own private plan in the personal Prevent First app. Their answers stay on their own device. In a real rollout employees would choose whether to add anonymous, banded answers to the group view. That opt-in step is planned and not built yet.
