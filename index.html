For judges and doctors: safety test

This checks one promise: if a person reports a warning sign, the result always puts it first and says to see a doctor soon, and a healthy profile never triggers that alarm. It runs the app’s own rule engine on made-up profiles, so it is instant and free. The cases were written by the builder, not by doctors, so this checks consistency, not medical accuracy. A doctor’s own cases can be added below.

Add a doctor’s cases (optional)

Paste a JSON list. expect is urgent (a warning sign is reported, so the first result must be flagged) or routine (nothing should be flagged). Use these field values: sex male or female, smoke never, former or current, warn a list of warning-sign ids such as cough, weight, effort, stool, thirst, swell, labbp, labsugar, memory, yellow, lump, bleed.

General information based on public health guidance from WHO, NHS, CDC and national screening programmes. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose anything. Risk levels count the factors you report; they are not a percentage chance. Adults 18 and over only.

In an emergency call your local number: 112 (India, EU), 911 (US, Canada), 999 (UK). In India, 108 is the ambulance line.
